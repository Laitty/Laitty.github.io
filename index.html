About me Explore research

I am Tianyou (Theo) Lai, an MS student in Artificial Intelligence Systems Management at Carnegie Mellon University, originally from Beijing. My research connects reliable learning systems, model evaluation, and human–AI interaction with applied machine learning in healthcare, industrial inspection, metasurface inverse design, forecasting, and energy systems.

In research, I work across the whole pipeline, from dataset preparation and model design to training and experimental evaluation. In industry, I have interned in startup data science, data engineering, quantitative research, consulting analytics, and product data.

laity [at] cmu [dot] edu

Research Interests

  • Model evaluation Whether a model’s answers, probabilities, and decisions hold up under controlled tests. ExposureBench · Choice2Seq · Which Differences Matter?
  • Applied machine learning Medical and dental AI, industrial defect detection, and forecasting for finance and energy. Oral Benchmark · TRACE · FREDNet · ReLaMix
  • Machine unlearning What unlearning actually removes, and what a model can still reason about afterward. Reasoning after Unlearning · Multimodal unlearning
  • Human–AI interaction How model outputs turn into decisions that people accept and act on. When Outputs Become Outcomes

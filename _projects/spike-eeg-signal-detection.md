As algorithm lead, I developed and optimized real-time detection algorithms for electrophysiological pulse signals from juvenile and adult rat Spike and EEG data.

The project supports symptom analysis and reverse translational treatment research through an ongoing collaboration with the School of Basic Medical Sciences, Peking University.

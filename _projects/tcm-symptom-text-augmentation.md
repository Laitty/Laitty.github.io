This research developed an LLM-driven approach for augmenting Traditional Chinese Medicine symptom texts, improving clarity, structure, and terminology for clinical record standardization and intelligent diagnostics.

I created a high-quality TCM dataset and designed specialized prompt engineering and fine-tuning. The approach reduced symptom text length by up to 44.1% and improved diagnostic accuracy by up to 28.4%. The work was recognized as an Honors Thesis.

This research focuses on federated learning with non-IID and heterogeneous data. The work includes a review of heterogeneity challenges and solutions invited by Expert Systems, along with research on a frequency-adaptive and semantic topology framework.

I led the design of a federated learning survey covering key challenges, algorithmic innovations, and case studies across healthcare, finance, and IoT. I also designed the Frequency-Adaptive Semantic Distillation framework, integrating semantic topology with dynamic frequency-based weighting.

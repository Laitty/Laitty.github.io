As a research assistant at Shenzhen University, I worked on advanced nuclear energy system data from the collaboration between China General Nuclear Power Group and Shenzhen University.

I collected and processed the data and designed an Informer-based time-series forecasting algorithm to support intelligent operation and maintenance. I also submitted work on Development of Rod-Bundle CHF Correlation Based on Data Augmentation for presentation at NUTHOS-15.

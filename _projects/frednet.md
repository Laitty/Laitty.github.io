FREDNet is a frequency and decomposed-spatial network for real-time surface defect detection. The work incorporated an Efficient Frequency Block, Decomposed Spatial Intra-scale Feature Interaction, and Hierarchical Attention Feature Fusion Network.

I led data curation for NEU-DET and GC-10, including preprocessing, formatting, and organizing data for training and evaluation. The work included benchmarking, ablation studies, and cross-domain generalization experiments, validating a 4.8% improvement in mAP@0.5.

This work is accepted at ICASSP 2026.

Research & Project Experience

My research connects reliable learning and model evaluation with human–AI interaction and applied machine learning.

Learning Systems and Human–AI Interaction

LLM Decision-Making, Reliability, and Unlearning

  • ExposureBench: Knowing When to Update — Evaluates when Web evidence selection should change a model’s probability and when disclosure makes the same policy change redundant. Crosses selection policies with information disclosure using exact finite-population targets and TREC DL retrieval with BM25 and Qwen reranking. Measures probability accuracy, informative updates, and conditional policy drift separately, using repeated calls and decision replay to assess noise and downstream consequences. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li

  • Choice2Seq — arXiv. Tests whether a model that selects the correct complete answer can also construct it through local sequential decisions. Sequence Composition Rate tracks that retention under serial construction: Jev keeps 33.0% and 2.1% of Native-correct arithmetic and lexical items despite 97.1% and 99.5% selection accuracy. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li
  • Which Differences Matter? — arXiv. Controlled tests of which rule and information changes guide decision-model answers: transfer on semantically separated rules, joint reliability on crossed rule–state tests, and directed use of table values versus mappings on Jev. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li
  • When Does Agreement Add Information? — Sensor and document verification with exact reference probabilities, varying copying versus independence so agreeing answers should update differently, then scoring both final probability accuracy and subsequent update correctness. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li
  • Reasoning after Unlearning — Evaluates original, unlearned, and target-excluded retrained Llama checkpoints on TOFU, separating context effects, answer exposure, and use of supplied answer information with matched controls, masking, and identity substitution. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li
  • Multimodal unlearning — Auxiliary supervision for unlearning in multimodal models, using extra training signals while target information is being removed. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li
  • Model selection feedback — Misleading comparison feedback in model selection, studying how distorted comparisons affect which model is chosen. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li

Human–AI Interaction and LLM Judges

  • When Outputs Become Outcomes — Distinguishes agreement with an assessment, acceptance of a judge’s authority, and subsequent action in livestream games and academic reviewing, using observational, interview, and presentation-comparison methods to examine source attribution and human mediation. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li

Federated and Heterogeneous Learning

  • FedSocratic — Verifiable questioning for federated language models, so questioning steps in the federated setting can be checked. Advisors: Prof. Qilei Li & Prof. David Camacho
  • After Cooperation Is Learned — arXiv preprint (2026). Gradient routing and optimizer-dependent maintenance of learned cooperation once multi-agent reinforcement learning has already acquired it. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li
  • MOSAIC — arXiv preprint (2026). Margin-oriented semantic–appearance interaction correction for personalized federated vision–language models, going beyond domain-level adaptation. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li
  • FedSAP — arXiv preprint (2026). Structured adaptive partitioning for federated learning on multi-domain heterogeneous edge devices. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li
  • Data heterogeneity survey — Expert Systems (2026). Led the survey framework on federated learning under non-IID and heterogeneous data, covering healthcare, finance, and IoT, and designed frequency-adaptive semantic distillation with semantic topology and dynamic frequency-based weighting. Advisors: Prof. Qilei Li & Prof. David Camacho

Applied Machine Learning

Healthcare and Clinical Language

  • TRACE — accepted at ACM MM 2026. Report supervision during training supports concept-based breast ultrasound diagnosis with image-only inference, jointly with Southern Medical University, connecting BI-RADS concepts, structured clinical descriptions, and concept-edit distillation. Advisors: Prof. Qilei Li & Prof. Zhenyuan Ning
  • Spike and EEG detection — RT-DETR and spatiotemporal graph methods for juvenile and adult rat electrophysiology, developed with Peking University for algorithm work and signal analysis in symptom studies.
  • LLM-based TCM symptom-text augmentation — Independent undergraduate honors thesis on dataset curation, prompting, and fine-tuning. Thesis experiments cut text length by up to 44.1%, raised terminology tokens 4.4-fold, and reached diagnostic accuracy of up to 81.3%. Advisor: Prof. Yi Yang
  • Dental orthodontic case retrieval — An ongoing intelligent case-retrieval project with Fujian Medical University for orthodontic records.

Industrial Inspection and Physical Inverse Design

  • FREDNet — ICASSP 2026. Frequency and decomposed-spatial learning with attention fusion for surface-defect detection, covering data curation, training, benchmarking, ablations, and cross-domain tests on NEU-DET and GC-10, with a 4.8% gain in mAP@0.5. Advisor: Lecturer Dr. Hongjuan Zhang
  • Metasurface inverse design — AIP Advances (2025). CNN–Transformer regression from polarization-conversion spectra with Gaussian-noise augmentation, reporting a mean MSE of 0.00331 on the design task. Advisor: Prof. Xiaodong He

Time-Series Forecasting and Energy Systems

  • Artificial Intelligence for Critical Heat Flux in Energy Systems: From Predictive Accuracy to Engineering Use (Shenzhen University, 2025.10–present) — Review proposal sent; now contacting Energy for an invitation to submit the critical-heat-flux survey. Advisor: Prof. Bo Pang
  • Mamba-In (Shenzhen University RA, 2025.10–2026.5) — Hybrid Mamba and Informer-style ProbSparse attention for long-horizon nuclear sensor forecasting, residual-based fault detection, and signal reconstruction on high-reliability monitoring streams. Advisor: Prof. Bo Pang
  • ReLaMix — arXiv preprint (2026). Residual bottleneck mixing for delayed financial observations, evaluated on second-resolution PAXGUSDT and BTCUSDT using non-overlapping temporal segments and delay-robustness benchmarks. Advisor: Prof. Qilei Li
  • Wind energy prediction — Energies (2025). Modular echo state networks, clustering, and training on turbine data, with a reported best R² of 0.9905. Advisor: Prof. Zhili Zhao

Early Research in Social Computing

  • Social computing in sports media (Sep.–Dec. 2023) — AI video generation, Python, Tableau, FreeD 3D, volumetric imaging, and AIGC used to study sports media and player competitiveness. Advisor: Lecturer Dr. Jian Zhan

I am Tianyou Lai, an MS student in Artificial Intelligence Systems Management at Carnegie Mellon University. I earned a BS in Data Science & Big Data Technology from Lanzhou University and completed an International Academy Exchange Program in Software Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin.

My research interests are Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, LLM, Data Science, and AI for Science. My work centers on time-series forecasting, signal detection, federated learning, and intelligent diagnostic systems, with applications in nuclear energy, healthcare, industrial monitoring, and financial time-series analysis.

Selected publications:

- TRACE: Training-time Report-guided and Clinically Ordered Concept Editing (ACMMM 2026, to appear)
- FREDNet: A Frequency and Decomposed-Spatial Network for Industrial Defect Signal Detection (ICASSP 2026)
- A Review of Federated Learning under Data Heterogeneity (Expert Systems, 2026)
- ReLaMix: Residual Latency-Aware Mixing for Delay-Robust Financial Time-Series Forecasting (arXiv, 2026)
- Deep Learning-Based Inverse Design of Broadband Metasurface Polarization Converter (AIP Advances, 2025)
- A Modularity-Enhanced Echo State Network for Nonlinear Wind Energy Predicting (Energies, 2025)

My professional experience includes data science, data engineering, quantitative research, tax and business consulting, and product data engineering internships.

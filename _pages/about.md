I am Tianyou Lai (Theo), from Xicheng, Beijing, an MS student in Artificial Intelligence Systems Management at Carnegie Mellon University. My research connects reliable learning systems, model evaluation, and human–AI interaction with applied machine learning in healthcare, industrial inspection, physical inverse design, forecasting, and energy systems.

In research, I work across model design, dataset preparation, training, and experimental evaluation. In industry, I have interned in product data, consulting analytics, quantitative research, startup data science, and data engineering.

I am Tianyou Lai, currently pursuing an MS in Artificial Intelligence Systems Management at Carnegie Mellon University (Aug. 2026 – Dec. 2027). I previously earned a BS in Data Science & Big Data Technology from Lanzhou University (Sep. 2021 – Jul. 2025), where I developed a strong foundation in machine learning, data mining, cloud computing, and big data systems.

My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, LLM, Data Science, and AI for Science, with an emphasis on time-series forecasting, signal detection, federated learning, and intelligent diagnostics. I have worked on predictive modeling for advanced nuclear energy systems, real-time EEG and spike signal analysis, and LLM-assisted symptom text augmentation in medical applications.

Selected publications and research highlights include:

- TRACE: Training-time Report-guided and Clinically Ordered Concept Editing (ACMMM 2026, to appear)
- FREDNet: A Frequency and Decomposed-Spatial Network for Industrial Defect Signal Detection (ICASSP 2026)
- A Review of Federated Learning under Data Heterogeneity (Expert Systems, 2026)
- ReLaMix: Residual Latency-Aware Mixing for Delay-Robust Financial Time-Series Forecasting (arXiv, 2026)

My industry experience spans data science, data engineering, and quantitative analysis. At Sichuan DeepGraph Intelligent Technology, I worked on data cleaning, relationship analysis, and algorithm optimization; at Lanzhou Bronze Ding Intelligent Technology, I processed large-scale datasets and supported deployment of data infrastructure; and at Guotai Junan Futures, I developed Python-based trading solutions and implemented TWAP/VWAP strategies. Earlier roles in tax and business consulting and product data engineering further strengthened my background in data processing, systems analysis, and applied intelligence.

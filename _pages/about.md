I am Tianyou Lai, an MS student in Artificial Intelligence Systems Management at Carnegie Mellon University. I earned a BS in Data Science & Big Data Technology from Lanzhou University, with coursework spanning machine learning, data mining, cloud computing, and big data analysis.

My research interests are Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, LLM, Data Science, and AI for Science. My work centers on time-series forecasting, signal detection, federated learning, and intelligent diagnostic systems, with applications in nuclear energy, healthcare, and industrial monitoring. I have developed predictive models for advanced nuclear system data, real-time spike and EEG signal analysis, and LLM-driven augmentation for TCM symptom text.

Selected research:

- TRACE: Training-time Report-guided and Clinically Ordered Concept Editing (ACMMM 2026, to appear)
- FREDNet: A Frequency and Decomposed-Spatial Network for Industrial Defect Signal Detection (ICASSP 2026)
- A Review of Federated Learning under Data Heterogeneity (Expert Systems, 2026)
- ReLaMix: Residual Latency-Aware Mixing for Delay-Robust Financial Time-Series Forecasting (arXiv, 2026)
